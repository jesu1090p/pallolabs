Child Safety Standards and Policy Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

At QuotePost, we are deeply committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for all our users. We have a zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

This policy outlines our standards and the strict actions we take to prevent, detect, and report any violations related to child safety on our platform, in compliance with international laws and platform guidelines (including Google Play policies).

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

QuotePost strictly prohibits the sharing, promotion, generation, or distribution of any content that depicts, encourages, or normalizes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Grooming behaviors or attempts to solicit minors for sexual purposes.
  • Content that sexualizes minors.
  • Any form of child endangerment or exploitation.

2. User Reporting and Flagging

We empower our community to help keep QuotePost safe. Users can report any suspicious or violating content directly within the app or by contacting our safety team.

  • In-App Reporting: Users can use the "Report" button located on the top right corner of any public post.
  • Direct Contact: Reports can also be sent directly to our dedicated safety team via our contact form at https://jesu1090p.github.io/pallolabs/contact or by email at rinconfutbolero82@gmail.com.

All reports involving potential CSAE are prioritized and reviewed immediately by our team.

3. Moderation and Detection

We employ proactive measures to prevent CSAM and CSAE from appearing on our platform.

  • Content Moderation: We use a combination of automated filtering tools and human moderation to detect and remove violating content.
  • Account Termination: Any account found to be engaging in, promoting, or distributing CSAE will be permanently suspended immediately and without warning.

4. Reporting to Law Enforcement and Authorities

We are legally and morally obligated to report any instances of CSAM or CSAE to the appropriate authorities.

If we become aware of any CSAM or severe child endangerment on our platform, we will preserve the relevant data and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local law enforcement agencies immediately.

We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations regarding child safety.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions about these safety standards, or if you need to report an urgent safety concern, please contact us immediately:

  • Email: rinconfutbolero82@gmail.com
  • Contact Form: https://jesu1090p.github.io/pallolabs/contact
  • Website: https://jesu1090p.github.io/pallolabs/

By using QuotePost, you agree to abide by these safety standards. We reserve the right to update this policy at any time to ensure the highest standards of safety for our community.
