Last updated: August 20, 2026

By using our application ("the Application", "QuotePost" or "Frases para Redes"), you agree to the information collection and use practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our Application has been designed with respect for your privacy. We do not require you to register or directly collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or phone number) so that you can use the core image editing and creation features.

For the operation of certain features, the Application interacts with the following services:

  • Local Storage: The Application requests storage permissions (or access to your gallery) exclusively so that you can save your final designs on your device or use your own photos as backgrounds. The images in your "Drafts" are saved locally on your phone.
  • Pexels API: We use the Pexels service to provide you with the online image bank. Your image search terms are sent anonymously to Pexels to return results. You can check the Pexels privacy policy on their official website.

2. Third-Party Service Providers (Ads and Analytics)

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in order to show personalized ads and report errors (crashes).

We use Google AdMob to show advertising. Google AdMob may collect and use advertising data (such as your device's advertising ID) and other anonymous information to show relevant ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses your data by visiting Google's privacy policy and terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. Since all your drafts and creations are saved locally on your own device, you are responsible for the physical security of your phone. We do not store your images on external servers.

4. Links to Other Sites

The Application may contain links to other websites (for example, when visiting the profiles of Pexels photographers). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. These external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
